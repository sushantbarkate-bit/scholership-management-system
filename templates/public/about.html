About the Scholarship Portal

This portal is a student-facing and administrative system for managing scholarship schemes for students studying in Maharashtra.

What it does
  • Stores student profiles and checks them against configurable eligibility rules.
  • Lets administrators publish schemes, define their required documents and review applications.
  • Keeps a full status history and notifies students at each step.
Important

Eligibility results shown here are preliminary. They are based on the information in your profile and the rules entered by the administrator. They are not an official government approval. Always follow the scheme provider's instructions and official notices.
